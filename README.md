# Water Reminder App
## Summary
Water Reminder App that helps people in Bahrain's hot weather to remember to drink water.

## 1. Problem
Many people forget to drink water during the day, especially in Bahrain's hot weather. Not drinking enough water causes tiredness, headaches, and dehydration.

## 2. AI Solution
A smart app that uses AI to calculate how much water each person needs. 
The app takes the person's weight and Bahrain's temperature, then sends a notification "Drink water now" to the phone to remind them.

## 3. Data
- Person's weight, age, and gender
- Temperature and humidity from Bahrain Meteorological Department website
- Number of cups of water the person drank today
